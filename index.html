We may not always say it, but we want you to know how thankful we are for everything you have done for us.

Thank you for every lesson, every reminder, every correction, and every moment you chose to be patient with us.

Thank you for believing that we could do better, even when we weren't sure ourselves.

Your classroom became more than a place where we learned. It became a place where we grew.

We will forget some lessons from the books, but we will remember how you made us feel.
